# BT-F01 Foundational Commitments and Axioms

2026-11-23. Version 1.0.

## Six commitments
1. No prescribed bubbles.
2. No preferred outcome.
3. No circular validation.
4. No automatic physics claims.
5. No assumed novelty.
6. Reproducibility.

## Axioms
A1: Finite distinguishable elements carry observable states.
A2: Interaction channels form a graph, not a priori coherent domains.
A3: Local evolution rules contain no bubble identities or boundaries.
A4: Relationships are measured read-only from recorded trajectories.
A5: Separate temporal observations allow persistence tests, without claims about fundamental time.

## BT-1
Conjecture: Some explicitly specified locally coupled systems produce at least two coherent persistent domains without predefined boundaries. Finite simulation cannot prove a general existence theorem.

## Operational definitions
Pairwise phase locking C(i,j,I)=abs(mean(exp(i*(theta_i-theta_j)))).
Internal coherence: mean C on edges within B. Boundary coherence: mean C on edges crossing B.
Distinction: internal minus boundary coherence.
Candidate bubbles: connected components of thresholded discovery-time graph satisfying fixed size constraints.
Validated domains: candidate sets passing tests on later, independent time windows.
Higher-order domains remain undefined. No physics claims are implied.
